### 🧠 Transfer Learning & Domain Generalization — ML Research

A group research project investigating how backbone layer freezing affects model accuracy and training efficiency across image-classification datasets and domains.

**Highlights**
- Conducted 90 controlled fine-tuning experiments using ResNet-50 and EfficientNet-B3
- Evaluated models across CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, and brain tumor MRI datasets
- Implemented configurable layer-freezing strategies and a PyTorch training pipeline
- Observed a reduction in training time of approximately 29% while CIFAR-10 accuracy decreased from 96.7% to 96.0%

**Focus:** PyTorch · Transfer Learning · Experimental Design · Model Evaluation
